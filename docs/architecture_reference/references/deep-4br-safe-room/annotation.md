## Why this plan works

- The safe room is one of the four bedrooms on the single-loaded corridor the narrow lot forces; it gets the same direct corridor access as every other bedroom, not a special isolated position. [J]
- The narrow lot's single-loaded corridor is a site-driven constraint the plan accepts openly rather than trying to disguise with an unsupported hub. [E]
- Sizing the safe room like an ordinary bedroom on this corridor keeps it usable as a bedroom day to day, with its mandated structural function layered on rather than replacing normal room proportions. [A]

## Trade-offs

- The safe room's reinforced-wall/door requirements interact with the narrow lot's already-tight width; there's less room to reposition it along the corridor than on a wider footprint, so its placement is more constrained than the safe-room entries on wider footprints in this set. [A]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Giving the safe room the same direct single-loaded-corridor access as every other bedroom, rather than tucking it behind another room, avoids [Room-in-a-room](../../anti_patterns.md#4-room-in-a-room).
