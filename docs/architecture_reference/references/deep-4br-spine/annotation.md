## Why this plan works

- A single-loaded corridor (rooms on one side only) keeps the other side of the lot free for a full-width rear garden; the circulation choice is a direct trade against private outdoor space, made deliberately. [O]
- Every one of the four bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms opens directly onto that single-loaded run, so the corridor still functions as genuine access rather than a room in a chain. [I]
- The narrow semi-detached lot leaves no width for a second wing or a hub, so the plan doesn't attempt one; massing matches what the site can actually hold. [O]

## Trade-offs

- Single-loading a long corridor to preserve the full-width garden means the corridor itself carries more circulation share than a double-loaded plan of the same program would; the garden gain is bought with hall length. [D]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Direct room-to-corridor access for all four bedrooms, rather than any bedroom serving as a route to another, avoids [Bedroom-to-bedroom access](../../anti_patterns.md#6-bedroom-to-bedroom-access).
