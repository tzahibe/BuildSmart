## Why this plan works

- Six bedrooms and four bathrooms fit a single narrow-deep footprint without any room's individual proportions being sacrificed to make space for the corridor; the program still lands as a near-square-ratio budget per room bay. [A]
- Every bedroom and bathroom opens directly onto the corridor, so despite its length the spine still functions as genuine circulation rather than a chain of rooms serving as each other's route. [I]
- Four bathrooms are distributed along the spine's length rather than clustered at one end, keeping wet-room reach from any bedroom to roughly one door away. [F]
- This entry is kept in the set specifically because it is the census's #1 measured quality gap made concrete, a long single spine with hall long/short median 9.4, giving the benchmark a real worst-case circulation-topology contrast to compare against the hub-lobby entries. [D]

## Trade-offs

- This entry's circulation topology is the deliberate negative case: the long single-loaded spine is the exact pattern rubric sections D and E flag as the largest measured real-corpus gap, kept here on purpose as a benchmark contrast rather than something to imitate.

## Anti-patterns avoided

- None at the circulation level: this entry deliberately represents [Hall spine without a compact hub](../../anti_patterns.md#2-hall-spine-without-a-compact-hub) for benchmark contrast rather than avoiding it. What it does avoid is [Bedroom-to-bedroom access](../../anti_patterns.md#6-bedroom-to-bedroom-access), since every bedroom still has its own direct door onto the spine rather than routing through a neighbour.
