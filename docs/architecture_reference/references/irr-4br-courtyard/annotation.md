## Why this plan works

- Three wings ring an inner courtyard, giving every wing's inward-facing rooms a second, private exterior exposure beyond the building's outer envelope. [C]
- Ring circulation around the courtyard means any room can be reached by at least two routes around the ring, rather than a single dead-end corridor per wing. [E]
- This organisation only appears on a large plot (frontage 20 m or more), exactly the site condition the 2 of 21 census plans that use it also required, so the massing choice tracks a real site precondition rather than being offered independent of plot size. [O]

## Trade-offs

- A courtyard plan needs a plot large enough to hold both the three wings and a courtyard of usable size in the middle; it is a large-plot-only solution, and the census shows it's genuinely rare (2 of 21) precisely because that site precondition is rare. [O]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Ring circulation, where any room has more than one route back to the entry, avoids the single-path structure that [Chained circulation](../../anti_patterns.md#11-chained-circulation) names.
