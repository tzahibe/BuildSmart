## Why this plan works

- The second wing is kept short, just long enough for two bedrooms and their shared bathroom, rather than extended to match the main wing's length for symmetry it doesn't need. [L]
- Both bedrooms share one bathroom placed directly on their short wing's own corridor, keeping that wing's internal circulation minimal. [F]
- At the smallest L in the set, the massing complexity is kept proportional to the smallest program too, a short second wing, not a full L on the scale of the larger entries. [L]

## Trade-offs

- Even a short second wing carries an L's structural seam cost; at only two bedrooms, a straight rectangle would have delivered the same program with one less exterior corner and one less seam to detail. [L]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Sizing the second wing to exactly the two-bedroom-plus-bathroom program, rather than offering it at a length the program doesn't fill, avoids [Unconditional L-massing slot](../../anti_patterns.md#10-unconditional-l-massing-slot).
