## Why this plan works

- At 100 sqm the plan still finds room for a half-bath in addition to the family bathroom, giving guests their own wet room without adding to the two bedrooms' shared bathroom traffic. [F]
- The rectangle stays a single simple volume at this size rather than adding a wing the small program doesn't justify. [L]
- Two bedrooms and one family bathroom keep the private wing short, so circulation between them stays a direct, minimal run. [D]

## Trade-offs

- A half-bath adds a second wet room to plumb and cluster against the family bathroom in a small footprint; worthwhile for guest convenience, but it is real added plumbing-adjacency work relative to the single-bathroom two-bedroom starter entry in this set. [F]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- The half-bath is sited to share plumbing proximity with the family bathroom rather than sitting off on its own, avoiding [Isolated wet room](../../anti_patterns.md#3-isolated-wet-room).
