## Why this plan works

- The private wing is organised around a compact room-lobby (~2.5-3.5 m), the exact hub-parti topology the 21-plan reference census shows as the dominant professional pattern, not a spine hall stretched to reach three bedrooms. [D]
- Because the lobby is a genuine junction rather than a corridor segment, the entrance sequence resolves into a clear branch point instead of a long hallway with doors down one side. [E]
- The shared family bathroom sits directly off the lobby, equally reachable from all three bedrooms rather than tucked behind one of them. [F]

## Trade-offs

- A dedicated hub-lobby only pays off when three or more rooms genuinely branch from it; at three bedrooms this plan is close to the minimum program size where the hub's circulation-area cost is worth its topology gain over a straight corridor. [D]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- The compact hub topology is exactly what closes the reference gap named in [Hall spine without a compact hub](../../anti_patterns.md#2-hall-spine-without-a-compact-hub); this entry is a direct positive example of the pattern that anti-pattern names the absence of.
