## Why this plan works

- The near-square footprint ratio keeps the massing close to the shape the rubric favors, avoiding the elongated envelope that would force wings into strip proportions. [A]
- The private wing organises its four bedrooms around a compact room-lobby (~2.5-3.5 m), the hub-parti topology the 21-plan census shows professional plans converge on, rather than a single long spine hall. [D]
- The guest WC sits directly off the entry, giving guest circulation its own wet-room access without routing visitors through the private wing. [H]
- The public zone, the guest WC and the private-wing hub each occupy their own zone, meeting only at the entry hall boundary rather than interleaving room by room. [J]

## Trade-offs

- A hub-lobby costs more circulation-adjacent wall than a straight corridor of the same length, and the lobby itself is pure circulation area that could otherwise be program area, the trade a compact hub makes for topology quality. [D]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- The compact room-lobby gives every bedroom a route that does not pass through another bedroom, avoiding [Hall spine without a compact hub](../../anti_patterns.md#2-hall-spine-without-a-compact-hub) and [Bedroom-to-bedroom access](../../anti_patterns.md#6-bedroom-to-bedroom-access).
