## Why this plan works

- The guest WC sits on the ground floor next to the entrance, so a visitor never has to go upstairs into the private bedroom level to find a bathroom. [H]
- The master ensuite is upstairs directly off the master bedroom, keeping that plumbing run as short as any single-level ensuite would be despite the second storey. [F]
- Ground floor and upper floor each carry a coherent, single-purpose program (public/guest vs. private bedrooms), meeting only at the stair rather than mixing room types level by level. [J]

## Trade-offs

- A guest WC on the ground floor and the family/ensuite bathrooms upstairs means the house's wet rooms are split across two levels by function rather than clustered as one adjacency group; good for guest access, but it caps how high a single plan's wet-room-adjacency share can ever reach. [F]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Placing the guest WC at the entrance instead of requiring visitors to find the upstairs bathrooms avoids an [Entrance dead-end](../../anti_patterns.md#7-entrance-dead-end)-style gap in what the arrival sequence actually offers.
