## Why this plan works

- The safe room is planned as a real bedroom first, sized and placed like any other bedroom in the private wing, with its mandated function layered on rather than carved out as leftover area. [A]
- It sits within the same private-wing corridor as the other three bedrooms, reachable the same way, rather than isolated at the building's structural core. [J]
- Labelling it "bedroom / security room" keeps both its everyday use and its regulatory use legible on the plan rather than hiding one behind the other. [K]

## Trade-offs

- A safe room's structural requirements (reinforced walls/door) constrain which bedroom can host it and where it can sit in the wing, a real siting constraint the other three bedrooms don't carry, which can push the room's proportions or door position away from what a free bedroom would get. [A]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Placing the safe room inside the ordinary private-wing corridor, with the same direct access as any other bedroom, avoids treating it as a [Room-in-a-room](../../anti_patterns.md#4-room-in-a-room) accessed only through another space.
