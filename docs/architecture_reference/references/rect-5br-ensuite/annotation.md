## Why this plan works

- The near-square footprint, even at 210 sqm, keeps room proportions close to the rubric's target band rather than stretching into a strip shape as the program grows. [A]
- Two bedrooms get their own ensuite, keeping each wet room's plumbing run short and directly adjacent to the bedroom it serves. [F]
- A separate guest WC keeps public/guest wet-room access off the private wing entirely, rather than sending visitors through the bedroom corridor. [J]
- The remaining three bedrooms share one family bathroom, keeping bathroom count proportional to the program without over-providing wet rooms the plumbing budget can't cluster well. [F]

## Trade-offs

- Two ensuites plus a family bathroom plus a guest WC is four wet rooms in one footprint, more plumbing runs to keep clustered than a single-family-bathroom plan, and the larger the count, the harder full wet-room adjacency is to hold at 85-90%. [F]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Each ensuite shares a wall directly with the bedroom it serves and the family bathroom/guest WC sit on the same private-wing corridor, keeping every wet room within reach of another fixture-bearing room rather than plumbing-isolated, avoiding [Isolated wet room](../../anti_patterns.md#3-isolated-wet-room).
