## Why this plan works

- The wide, shallow footprint matches a broad, shallow plot directly rather than forcing a deep plan onto a lot that doesn't have the depth for one. [O]
- With only two bedrooms, the plan keeps the shared bathroom equally close to both rather than favouring one bedroom's access over the other. [F]
- The shallow depth keeps every room close to an exterior wall, since there is little interior depth for a room to be landlocked in. [C]

## Trade-offs

- A wide, shallow footprint on a broad plot gives frontage-facing rooms generous exposure, but it also spreads the plot's usable side-yard and rear-yard area thin along the width, a site trade-off this footprint family accepts by design. [O]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Shallow depth keeps every habitable room within reach of an exterior wall, so this footprint structurally avoids the [Windowless habitable room](../../anti_patterns.md#9-windowless-habitable-room) risk that a deep, landlocked plan would carry.
