## Why this plan works

- Both bedrooms sit upstairs, keeping the entire ground floor free for the public zone and guest half-bath rather than splitting the small footprint's ground level between public and private program. [J]
- At 115 sqm over two levels, the plan doesn't add a hub or a second wing it has no program to justify, massing stays as simple as the smallest single-level entries in this set. [L]
- With only two bedrooms upstairs, the landing serves as a short, direct junction rather than a long corridor. [D]

## Trade-offs

- Putting both bedrooms upstairs on a small footprint means the whole private program depends on one stair and one small upper landing; there's no redundancy in vertical circulation the way a larger multi-level plan might have. [M]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- A short, two-door upstairs landing rather than a stretched corridor keeps this small two-storey plan from degenerating into [Hall spine without a compact hub](../../anti_patterns.md#2-hall-spine-without-a-compact-hub) even at its reduced scale.
