## Why this plan works

- The wide, shallow footprint puts the public zone across the full street frontage, giving living/dining/kitchen the widest, most open volume the plot allows instead of squeezing them into a narrow front bay. [G]
- Bedrooms sit in columns behind the front band, reached by one corridor running the depth of the plot, direct, without doubling back through the public zone. [D]
- The front band gives every public room genuine street-facing exposure at the plan's widest dimension, rather than relying on side or rear walls only. [C]

## Trade-offs

- Putting the whole public zone on the front band means every bedroom is set back behind it; all private-wing access has to route around or behind the public band, adding to the corridor's total length. [D]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Bedroom columns reached by their own corridor, not by passing through the public front band, avoid the public zone being a mandatory route to the bedrooms, a version of [Chained circulation](../../anti_patterns.md#11-chained-circulation).
