## Why this plan works

- The flex room sits directly off the entrance rather than inside the private wing, so using it as a home office doesn't require routing clients or visitors past the bedrooms. [H]
- Because it's reached from the entry rather than from the bedroom hub, the flex room adds a fifth room to the program without adding a door to the private wing's circulation. [J]
- The wide footprint gives the flex room its own bay at the front of the plan, distinct from both the public zone and the bedroom wing. [L]

## Trade-offs

- A flex room placed at the entrance uses frontage area that could otherwise widen the public zone or shorten the bedroom wing's corridor; it is program flexibility bought with the plan's most valuable, most visible area. [L]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Reaching the flex room directly from the entrance, rather than through the public zone or the private wing, avoids folding it into either zone's circulation as a [Chained circulation](../../anti_patterns.md#11-chained-circulation) waypoint.
