## Why this plan works

- At 12.5 m or more of frontage, the plot supports a genuine side-by-side bedroom-wing regime, two bedroom columns in parallel rather than one column stretched to fit four rooms end to end. [D]
- The guest WC sits near the entrance, off the public front band, so visitors never need private-wing access to find a bathroom. [H]
- Side-by-side wings keep both bedroom columns the same short depth, instead of letting one column grow long to reach the fourth bedroom. [A]

## Trade-offs

- Side-by-side wings need two parallel corridors (or a doubled hub) rather than one, more circulation area in absolute terms than a single-column plan of the same bedroom count, in exchange for keeping each column short. [D]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Two short parallel columns instead of one long one keep every bedroom close to a landing point, avoiding the long single spine named in [Hall spine without a compact hub](../../anti_patterns.md#2-hall-spine-without-a-compact-hub).
