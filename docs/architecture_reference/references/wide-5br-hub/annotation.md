## Why this plan works

- Five bedrooms open onto one compact room-lobby, the hub-parti topology the census shows professional plans converge on, instead of a spine long enough to reach five doors in sequence. [D]
- Three bathrooms are distributed around the hub so bedrooms on either side of it reach a wet room in roughly the same number of steps. [F]
- The wide footprint gives the hub room to sit centrally rather than being pushed to one end of a narrow plan, keeping every branch roughly the same length. [E]

## Trade-offs

- Five to six doors converging on one hub is close to the practical ceiling for a single compact lobby before it starts functioning more like a small room than a junction; this entry represents that upper bound, not a typical case. [D]

## Anti-patterns avoided

- A genuine multi-door hub, not a corridor stretched to reach five rooms, avoids [Hall spine without a compact hub](../../anti_patterns.md#2-hall-spine-without-a-compact-hub) even at this program size.
