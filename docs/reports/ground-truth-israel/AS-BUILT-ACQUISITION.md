# The route to field evidence — what exists, who holds it, and the smallest thing that would get it

**Issue:** #269 (ROOT #262, condition **C2**) · **2026-10-28** · **Nothing was sent. Nobody was
contacted.** The draft message is `OUTREACH-HE.md`; it is a draft.

## Which project to pursue, and why it is A

**Nahariya 17/2026** is the target, and not because its documents are the richest — Ramat Gan's are
(`CANDIDATE-MATRIX.md`). It is the target because it is the only candidate where **the field
evidence is contractually required to exist**:

| what | where it is written | who signs it |
|---|---|---|
| the actual quantities, fixed by measurement on site | §61.2 — `הכמויות שבוצעו בפועל ... תיקבענה על סמך מדידות שתעשנה על ידי המפקח והקבלן` | supervisor **and** contractor |
| a **measurement book** — `ספר המדידות` or dedicated schedules | §61.2 — `כל המדידות תירשמנה בספר המדידות או ברשימות מיוחדות לכך ותיחתמנה על ידי המפקח והקבלן` | supervisor and contractor, **every page** |
| a re-measurement on challenge, then a binding determination | §61.5 — the contractor may object in writing within 7 days; a second measurement follows; the `מנהל` then determines finally | — |
| **quantity calculations and sketches for every item** | final-account appendix §6.1.1 — `חישובי כמויות וסקיצות לכל סעיף, חתומים בכל עמוד על ידי הקבלן והמפקח עם סיכום כמויות, חישוב ותאריך` | contractor and supervisor, every page, dated |
| **`AS-MADE`** | §6.2.1 — a condition of the final account | contractor, supervisor **and a licensed surveyor — `מודד מוסמך`** |
| handover: designers' approval and handover protocol | §6.2.3 — `אישור מתכננים, אישור מסירה ופרוטוקול מסירה` | designers |

So for this one project the chain **estimate → measured quantity → as-made → signed final account**
is specified end to end. By contrast Ramat Gan has abolished the middle of that chain in writing
(`ולא תבוצע מדידת כמויות`) and Nahariya 19/2026 was cancelled before it began.

Two further reasons A is the practical target: the whole job is **₪534,171.62 before VAT**
(₪630,322.51 with VAT) as its own summary page states, so a single final account is a tractable
document rather than a construction archive; and bids closed **09/04/2026**, so a job of this size
is plausibly complete or near complete by now, which means the as-made may already exist.

## The one independent measurement anywhere in this corpus

ROOT #262's C2 asks for evidence of **the built structure**, and the L2/L3 distinction in
`CANDIDATE-MATRIX.md` turns on independence: a final account is measured by the supervisor and the
contractor, and **both are parties to the payment it determines**. An audited number is better than
an estimate, but it is not a disinterested instrument.

There is exactly one disinterested mechanism in all three tenders, and it is in A:

> §23.6 — `יובהר כי אם יתגלו הפרשי מדידה משמעותיים, על פי החלטת המנהל, בין הסימונים ומדידות שיבוצעו
> על ידי הקבלן, ימונה **מודד בקרה** על חשבון הקבלן בעלות של 1% מערך החוזה`

A **control surveyor**, appointed when measurement differences are found to be material, at 1 % of
the contract value and at the contractor's expense. That is a third party measuring the building
precisely because the first two disagreed about it — which is the nearest thing to an independent
field measurement found anywhere in this Issue.

**It therefore changes the ask.** The ideal acquisition is not "a completed project" but **a
completed project on which a control surveyor was appointed** — because there the measurement
exists *twice*, taken by parties with opposed interests, and the difference between the two
readings is itself the accuracy bound that #263 searched six avenues for and never found. A single
such file would, on its own, carry what no public dataset anywhere publishes: a construction
quantity with a method and a disagreement. Whether any such appointment has ever been made is
**unknown and unverifiable from outside** — it is the first thing the outreach asks.

## Which items would actually be measured, and which would not

A final account is not a takeoff of everything. Within A's own bill the mixture is visible:

- **measured by area or length, so re-measurable**: the gypsum partitions (151.00 m², convention stated), the acoustic ceiling (114.00 m²), the parquet (120.00 m²), the external paint (294.00 m²), the ceramic cladding (7.20 m²), the flooring patches (2.00 m²).
- **priced as `מכלול` or `קומפלט`, so never measured**: a long run of clauses in sub-chapter 1.30 whose quantity column reads `מכלול` — cleanliness, nuisance limitation, material-handling conditions, equivalence approvals, testing and certification, structural certification — plus skips, waste clearance and the accessibility chapter at a flat ₪35,000.
- **contingent**: `03.1.340` self-levelling screed, 10.00 m², `במידת הצורך יאושר ע"י מפקח` — built in full, in part or not at all.

Two consequences for C5's re-run. First, **the four materials ROOT #262 names map onto the
measured group, not the lump group** — flooring, ceiling, paint and skirting are all area or length
items — so a final account would in principle carry exactly the rows we want. Second, **the
reference set will be small**: on A perhaps six rows, and skirting will not be among them because
`BOQ-EXTRACTION.md` found no skirting item in this bill at all.

## What to ask for, itemised

So that the draft stays short, the full list belongs here rather than in the message:

1. `חשבון סופי` for one completed project, **with the amounts redacted if preferred** — the quantities are what matters, not the money.
2. `חישובי כמויות וסקיצות לכל סעיף` per §6.1.1 — **the sketches are the item**: they are where a professional's measurement is written down against a geometry.
3. `ספר המדידות` or the §61.2 schedules, signed.
4. `תכניות AS-MADE` per §6.2.1, with the licensed surveyor's signature block.
5. **Which items were measured after construction and which were paid as `מכלול`/`קומפלט`** — stated by the holder, not deduced by us.
6. **Whether a `מודד בקרה` was ever appointed under §23.6 on any project, and if so his measurement.**
7. The measurement convention actually applied where the bill is silent — above all **the wall-height datum** and **whether skirting was measured through door openings and deducted, or broken at them**.
8. For Ramat Gan separately: a re-share of the two drawing galleries, and `רשימת הגמרים` and sections `200`/`201`.

Items 5, 6 and 7 are the ones a document dump would not answer. They are the reason the ask is a
question to a person and not a download.

## The honest ceiling of this whole route

Paths 1 and 3 reach **L2**. L2 is a large improvement on everything this ROOT has had — it is the
first quantity measured against something built — and **it is still not C2**, because the measurers
are the two parties to the payment. Only a control surveyor's reading (path 1, if one exists) or our
own tape (path 5) reaches independence. Stated plainly so it cannot be mistaken later: **acquiring
a final account would not, by itself, let any accuracy claim be made.** It would let a *convention-
stated* comparison be made against a professional's post-construction measurement, which is the
narrower and defensible claim #267 already said is the only one available.
