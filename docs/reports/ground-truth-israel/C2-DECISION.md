## The eight questions

**1. Which candidate has the best drawings and BOQ?**
Split, and the split matters. **A has the best evidence in hand** — the only candidate whose figured,
scaled, dated, authored drawing *and* professional priced bill are both openly downloadable and both
readable, and the one that yielded three real-world measurement conventions. **C has the best
evidence that exists** — plaster split wall-from-ceiling, skirting as its own linear item, cladding
attributed to room types, masonry by thickness, an opening schedule by mark, a finishes schedule and
dedicated flooring and ceiling plans, plus a 308-page specification that states its conventions and
**overrides the national one in writing**. C's drawings could not be opened and its contract has
abolished measurement; A's bill and drawing describe different scopes. **B is last** and still
useful: the only subject that figures a wall height.

**2. Which quantity categories are independently recomputable?**
**None.** Of 20 candidate quantities across six categories and three projects: `COMPUTABLE` **0**,
`CONVENTION_DEPENDENT` **3**, `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` **13**, `SOURCE_CONTRADICTION` **4**. The three
convention-dependent ones are the nearest misses — A's suspended ceiling, B's external envelope, C's
skirting — and each is blocked by a choice the documents decline to make rather than by absent data.
Exactly one reached an actual comparison: B's envelope, where **two conventions × two datums produce
four answers spanning 135.50–151.24 m²** around a reference of 145.00.

**3. Which dimensions or conventions are missing?**
Missing dimensions: **wall height** (absent from all three bills; present only as a price band, a
hanger depth, soffit annotations and B's elevations), **the plan shape of B**, and **every sheet of
C**. Missing conventions, each demonstrably decisive: the **wall-height datum** (A's soffit sits up
to 1.0 m above its ceiling; B's sheet figures 4.00 and levels +4.45 in the same place); **opening
deduction** (worth 4.6 % of B's envelope); **the skirting break** at doors (C states a length and
not the rule, which is #267's −23.94 %); **gross versus finished face** (A's note 3 fixes the
drawing as `ברוטו - ללא חיפוי גמר` while its ceiling trims to the finished wall); and **whether
figures or the drawing govern** — A answers this and the answer is neither, `אין לקבוע מידות עפ"י
שרטוט. המידות המחייבות הן מידות שנמדדו בשטח לאחר ביצוע`. One genuine positive: the national
`אופני מדידה` these bills defer to is **published free** by the Ministry of Defence, chapters 04,
09, 10, 11 and 22, so the *default* conventions are retrievable — while ג'2 09.6(ד) proves a tender
may override the default in writing, so the default is never the answer.

**4. Are any quantities independently verified against the built structure?**
**No. Not one, in any of the three.** Every quantity is pre-construction. A's project has a
contractual chain to field evidence — §61.2's signed `ספר המדידות`, §6.1.1's per-item quantity
calculations and sketches, §6.2.1's `AS-MADE` signed by contractor, supervisor **and a licensed
`מודד מוסמך`** — and **none of it is published**. B was cancelled before anything was built. C's
contract states the building quantities will never be measured.

**5. What prevents C2 from passing?**
Three things, in ascending order of how much they matter.
*Third:* **licensing** — all three prohibit redistribution, so nothing may enter the repository. This
is a real constraint and the smallest, because facts may be cited.
*Second:* **the L1→L2 gap** — the field evidence is not missing from the world, it is missing from
the *published* pack. For A it is contractually required to exist and held privately; for C the
contract abolished it.
*First, and new:* **no bill item is bound to a drawn room.** Not one of the 24 extracted quantities
cites a sheet, a grid reference or a room; C's nine attributions are to room *types* behind which
the register shows four separate layouts. A takeoff is a per-room measurement **summed**, and a
tender publishes the sum. ROOT #262 recorded that three of the four materials need only plan-view
geometry plus door widths, and **that is true and it was not what blocked this**: A had the
plan-view geometry, B had the wall height, C had the per-room takeoff, and each failed for a
different reason, none of them the height. **A dataset is not "plans plus quantities" — it is plans
plus quantities plus the mapping between them, and the mapping is the artefact nobody publishes.**

**6. Who can provide the missing evidence?**
Four parties, and the first is reachable today at no cost.
- **The client who holds the final account.** For A that is **החברה הכלכלית נהריה בע"מ**, whose project manager's address is published: **סמיון טוקר, `semyont@nahariya.muni.il`**. Under §6.1.1 and §6.2.1 they must hold the quantity calculations, the sketches and the signed as-made.
- **A `מודד בקרה` under §23.6** — a control surveyor appointed at 1 % of contract value when measurement differences prove material. **This is the only disinterested measurement mechanism in the entire corpus**, and it exists precisely because two interested parties disagreed. A file with one in it carries what no public dataset on earth publishes: a construction quantity with a method **and a disagreement**, which is the accuracy bound #263 searched six avenues for.
- **The supervisor and the contractor jointly**, who sign the measurement book — but both are parties to the payment it determines, so they reach **L2**, not L3.
- **Ourselves, with a tape** — #263 already identified this and it remains the only route under our own control that reaches L3.

Free, a published address, no registration, no fee, no commitment. Approval is needed only because

A **second, even smaller** action is available and worth bundling: Draft 2 to

**8. Continue toward an automated quantity product, remain blocked, or investigate a narrower professional-assistance tool?**

**Investigate the narrower professional-assistance tool. Hold the quantity product open, do not
close it, and do not build toward it yet.**

ROOT #262's pre-stated NO-GO fires *if C2 proves unobtainable*. **It has not been proved
unobtainable** — it is unobtained, with two free routes untried — so the NO-GO does not fire and
closing the direction now would be the mirror of the error this ROOT exists to prevent. But three
findings point the same way:

- **#267 had already narrowed the claim** from accuracy against the truth to accuracy against a *stated convention*. This Issue shows that even that narrower claim **cannot be validated on public data**, because validating it needs the per-room mapping, and the mapping is private by the structure of the industry rather than by accident.
- **The same evidence describes an assistance market precisely.** The professional already does this work by hand, already holds the mapping, already resolves the conventions per job, and already produces the artefact — `חישובי כמויות וסקיצות לכל סעיף`, signed on every page. Three of the four materials need only plan-view geometry plus door widths, which the product could help a professional with **today**, with the professional supplying the eight facts and owning the convention choices. That is C4's re-scope clause arriving with evidence behind it.
- **The free national convention anchor cuts the same way.** The Blue Folder's `אופני מדידה` is a gift to a tool whose user states a convention and a near-irrelevance to a tool that must choose one unaided — and ג'2 09.6(ד), `בניגוד לאמור במפרט הכללי`, shows a real job overriding the national default in writing.

One fact must sit beside all of this, because it bounds every option. #257 established that **no
plan ingestion exists and never did**, so comparison B remains NOT APPLICABLE. This Issue adds a
measured sense of what ingestion would face on exactly this data: A's architectural plan and its
details file are Konica Minolta scans with **zero text characters**, and in the bills that *do* have
a text layer, **digits embedded in right-to-left runs extract in reversed order** — `250` arrives as
`052` and `3.5` as `5.3`. Both were worked around here by rendering the page and reading it, and by
reading the numeric columns separately. Neither is exotic and neither is free.
